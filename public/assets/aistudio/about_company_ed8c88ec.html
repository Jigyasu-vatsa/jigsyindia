Our Story & Mission

JIGYASU INTEGRATED GLOBAL SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED, or JIGSY INDIA, is a leading provider of innovative and comprehensive solutions across multiple sectors. Our core mission is to empower individuals and organizations through cutting-edge technology, skill development, and professional services. We are dedicated to delivering excellence and fostering a connected, skilled, and prosperous global community.

The vision for this company was initially conceived on October 3rd, 2010. In honor of that significant day, the founder officially declared it the first foundation day for work commencement and engagement in the year 2025.

Our Vision

  • To be a global leader in integrated services, transforming industries and unlocking human potential through curiosity, wisdom, and professional excellence.
  • Achieve global acceptance through the joint contribution of our team, empowering quick and effective market adaptations.
  • Focus on nurturing upcoming generations with innovative, future-ready technologies for a smart, modernized era.
