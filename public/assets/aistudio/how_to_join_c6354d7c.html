How to Join JIGSY

For Jobseekers

1. **Register Your Account**: Click on the 'Join' button and fill out your details. You'll receive a one-time password (OTP) via email to verify your account.

2. **Get Your Unique ID**: After successful verification, we will provide you with a unique JIGSY ID. This is your professional identity on our platform.

3. **Upload Your Resume**: Go to your dashboard and upload your latest resume. Our system will analyze it to help you find the best jobs.

4. **Browse, Apply & Track**: Search our extensive job listings. You can filter by sector to find jobs that match your skills. Apply with a single click! Track status in dashboard, be aware with updates through dashboard notification, shortlisting, interview updates and further communications.

For Clients

1. **Register as a Client**: Use the 'Join' button and select the client role. Verify your email to create your account and get your unique ID.

2. **Submit a Request**: Log in to your client dashboard and submit a formal request for your project needs, whether it's for consultancy, advertising, or skilled professionals.

3. **Manage Communication**: Our admin team will review your request and contact you. You can view all communications through client dashboard and with dedicated chat services, shared documents, and meeting schedules directly on your dashboard.

4. **Partner with Us**: Collaborate with our expert teams and benefit from our integrated services designed to help your business grow.
