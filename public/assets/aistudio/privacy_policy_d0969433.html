Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2025

1. Introduction and Scope

Jigyasu Integrated Global Services Private Limited (“JIGSY INDIA”, “we”, “us”, “our”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we **collect, use, process, and protect** your Personal Information when you use our website, mobile applications, and all related services (collectively, "Services"). By using our Services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity & Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, physical address, and user profile data provided during registration, application, or inquiry submission.
  • Professional Data: Resumes, employment history, educational background, and skill sets submitted for job/internship applications.
  • Transaction Data: Details about payments to and from you, and other details of products or services you have purchased from us (though financial details are handled by secure third-party payment gateways).
  • Technical Data: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, time zone setting and location, operating system, and platform.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our Services, including pages viewed, content interacted with, and time spent on the site.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Any content you submit to the site, such as reviews, testimonials, forum posts, or feedback.

3. Legal Basis and Use of Your Data

We only process your Personal Information when we have a **legal basis** to do so. Our primary purposes for using your data include:

  • **Performance of a Contract:** To register you as a user, provide the services you requested (e.g., software access, training), and process your job/internship applications.
  • **Legitimate Interests:** To ensure the security of our Services (e.g., fraud prevention, user verification), improve our offerings, and personalize your experience.
  • **Legal Obligation:** To comply with applicable laws and regulations (e.g., tax, audit, and employment laws).
  • **Consent:** Where required, we may seek your specific consent to process data for marketing purposes.
  • **Communication:** To send essential service updates, offers, and respond to your support queries.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your Personal Information with third parties under the following circumstances:

  • Affiliates: With our subsidiaries or companies under common control for operational purposes.
  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors who perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing, IT and system administration, and email delivery. These parties are contractually prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.
  • Legal and Regulatory: When legally required to do so, such as in response to a court order, subpoena, or to comply with applicable laws.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with or during negotiations of any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business by another company.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses **cookies and similar tracking technologies** to enhance functionality, analyze usage, and gather demographic information. This includes **Strictly Necessary**, **Analytical**, and **Functionality** cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. For a detailed list of the cookies we use and how you can manage them, please consult our separate **Cookie Policy** [Link to Cookie Policy Page].

6. Data Security and Retention

We have implemented appropriate **technical and organizational measures** to prevent your Personal Information from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. Sensitive data, such as passwords, are encrypted using industry-standard hashing algorithms.

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including for satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. For applicants, data is generally retained for a period of [e.g., 24 months] unless you become an employee.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your Personal Information, including the right to:

  • **Access:** Request a copy of the Personal Information we hold about you.
  • **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion or removal of your Personal Information.
  • **Object:** Object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • **Portability:** Request the transfer of your Personal Information to you or to a third party.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 11.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed toward minors under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child under 18 has provided us with Personal Information, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new **“Effective Date.”** Your continued use of the Services after the effective date of the revised Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the terms.

10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy and its interpretation shall be governed by the **laws of the Republic of India**. Any disputes arising under this Policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in **Darbhanga, Bihar**, where JIGSY INDIA is registered.
